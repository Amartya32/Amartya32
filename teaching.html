Courses

  • 2021–2026

    Cybathlon Course

    Course and Module Leader, MIRMI, Technical University of Munich

    I designed and lead this project-based module, which enrols 50–70 students each semester from engineering, computer science and biomedical disciplines. Students work in interdisciplinary teams to develop and build upper-limb wearable assistive devices, such as prostheses and robotic hands, for competitive evaluation at the international Cybathlon (ETH Zurich, 2024).

    Topics covered:

    • Biomechanics of the upper limb and activities of daily living
    • Assistive device and prosthetic design
    • Control systems for wearable robotics
    • Clinical evaluation and human factors

    I'm responsible for the full teaching cycle: curriculum design, lectures and technical workshops, team supervision, assessment, mentoring, and course review based on student feedback.

    Funding: MIRMI, TU Munich, intramural award (2025), with me as Principal Investigator.
