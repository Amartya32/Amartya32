  • 2019–2020

    AGILIS: Neural Stimulation for Hand Grasp

    Co-Investigator, University of Heidelberg (advisor: Prof. Katja Mombaur)

    A clinical trial of multipolar electrical stimulation of the median and radial nerves, using minimally invasive implanted electrodes to restore functional hand grasp. I developed upper-limb musculoskeletal hand models, hand-tracking protocols and motion capture workflows for clinical data collection with people with C4–C6 tetraplegia, as part of a France–Germany collaboration.

    Funded by EIT Health, European Union. Sponsor: Assistance Publique – Hôpitaux de Paris.

    ClinicalTrials.gov: NCT04306328

  • 2017–2018

    ATLAS 2030 paediatric exoskeleton

    Innovation Associate (Head of R&D), Marsi Bionics, Spain

    I led research and development for the ATLAS 2030, an exoskeleton that helps children walk.

    • Founded and built the HUBOTIK Laboratory, now an established biomechanics research centre serving children and older people in the Madrid region
    • Developed the CE marking pathway for Class II wearable medical devices, supporting FDA certification readiness
    • Set up a clinical gait laboratory and enabled pre-clinical trials using motion capture
    • Improved the exoskeleton's ergonomics, balance and adaptability across several paediatric conditions

    Featured in El País, November 2018.

    SenseBack: Real-time musculoskeletal modelling for upper-limb bionics

    Postdoctoral Research Associate, Keele University, UK (advisor: Prof. Edward Chadwick)

    • Designed and ran experimental and clinical protocols
    • Helped coordinate work across the six UK institutions
