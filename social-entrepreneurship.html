EnHands e.V.

  • 2022–present

    Enabling Accessible Prosthetic Hands

    Co-Founder, not-for-profit organisation

    We develop and distribute affordable upper-limb prostheses to amputees in underserved communities, to combat social exclusion and stigma.

    • Devices given free of charge to people in Nepal, Bangladesh and Pakistan
    • Field deployment with our partner Naya Qadam at an amputee camp in Bardibas, Nepal (2023)
    • Field deployment with Naya Qadam in Bangladesh (2026)

    Funding partners: TU Munich, TUM Venture Labs, Vodafone, TU Dresden, UnternehmerTUM, Naya Qadam and CeTI.
